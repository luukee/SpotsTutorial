Email and password boxes on the app
What to enter in each spotsymptoms.org signup box
Box on the app What to enter
Username The Username from REDCap for that person. Do not invent a new one.
Email The email they will use to sign in.
Password One the family creates.

Google sign-in has no Username box — they use Sign in with Google with the matching email. Still give password families the Username from REDCap.

The main gotcha: REDCap’s Primary email and the Email box on spotsymptoms.org are not the same thing. In REDCap, Primary email is always the parent’s, because that’s where study messages go. The website Email is just how someone logs in. So a child can sign up with their own email on the site while REDCap still has the parent’s email as Primary email on their record. Mixing those two up is what trips people up.
